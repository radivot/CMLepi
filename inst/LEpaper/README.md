## Diagnosis of Chronic Myeloid Leukemia at a Mean Age of 59 Implies a Mean Loss of Roughly 8 of 24 Years of Life in the United States

To setup SEER and mortality data first run the scripts on the main [page](https://github.com/radivot/CMLepi) and `mkMorts.R`. 
It should then be possible to run all of the other scripts, though `S7_mkhBack.r` must come before `S7_RP.R`.

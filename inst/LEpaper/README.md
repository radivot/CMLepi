# Diagnosis of Chronic Myeloid Leukemia at a Mean Age of 59 Implies a Mean Loss of Roughly 8 of 24 Years of Life in the United States


This folder holds all of the R scripts that were used to generate the figures in this paper. The scripts 'mkSEER.R' and 'mkMorts.R' 
should be run first.  After that the order of execution should not matter.  Scripts generating Figures 1, 2 and 4 were already
presented in the main 'README.md' [file](https://github.com/radivot/CMLepi).
